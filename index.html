Meet Dr. Maya Bennett

Less fixing. More understanding.

I’m a clinical psychologist who believes good therapy should make you feel more like yourself—not like a project that needs fixing.

My work blends relational therapy, CBT, and mindfulness-based practices. I’m warm but direct, curious without being intrusive, and always interested in the person behind the problem.

Sessions are collaborative. We’ll make room for insight, but we’ll also talk about Tuesday afternoon when your thoughts start racing.
